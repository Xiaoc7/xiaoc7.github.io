I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC), advised by Prof. Wen Li ↗.

Previously, I received my B.Eng. from the School of Computing and Artificial Intelligence at Southwest Jiaotong University (SWJTU). During my undergraduate studies, I was fortunate to work with Prof. Xiaolin Wu ↗, from whom I received valuable research training.

My research focuses on 3D reconstruction and generative models, especially geometry-aware diffusion, feed-forward neural rendering, and scalable 3D representations.
